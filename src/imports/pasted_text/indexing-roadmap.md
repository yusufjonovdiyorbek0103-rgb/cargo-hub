Help Center

Main content:

Create search bar:
Search help topics

FAQ categories:

- Policies

FAQ items:

For Authors:
Q: How do I submit a manuscript?
A: Create an account, start a new submission, complete all required metadata, upload files, confirm declarations, and submit the manuscript through the online portal.

Q: Can I submit by email?
A: Manuscripts should be submitted through the online submission system. Email submission is not normally accepted.

Q: What languages are accepted?
A: CAJAIDT accepts manuscripts in English, Uzbek, and Russian. English title, abstract, and keywords are required for all manuscripts.

Q: Is there a publication fee?
A: Publication fees are to be announced. There is no submission fee.

For Reviewers:
Q: How do I accept a review invitation?
A: Log in to the reviewer dashboard and select Accept or Decline for the assigned manuscript.

Q: Can I use AI tools while reviewing?
A: Reviewers must not upload confidential manuscript materials into external AI tools unless explicitly permitted by the journal and appropriate confidentiality safeguards are in place.

For Publication:
Q: Is the journal indexed?
A: CAJAIDT is currently in development. Indexing applications are planned after the journal establishes publication history and required standards.
